## Classification review

Reviewed on October 8, 2026 by the implementer and an independent reviewer. The evidence supports one comparable **six-digit 090121 interval from 2021-09 through 2026-08**, for roasted coffee, not decaffeinated, in both directions. This is an evidence-based classification conclusion, not a claim that every ten-digit code stayed unchanged or that trade values have been verified.

The [Census reference index](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/reference/index.html) provides annual import and export concordances. We retained all twelve 2021–2026 `imp-code.txt`/`exp-code.txt` files under ignored `data/raw/classification-090121/`. Their source URLs, SHA256 hashes and matching descriptions are recorded in [the evidence extract](090121-classification-evidence.json), independently checked against the raw files. All matching descriptions stay within roasted, non-decaffeinated coffee. All six export Chapter 9 PDFs retain the same parent and organic/other children: [2021](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2021/c09.pdf), [2022](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2022/c09.pdf), [2023](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2023/c09.pdf), [2024](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2024/c09.pdf), [2025](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2025/c09.pdf), [2026](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2026/c09.pdf).

The import concordances contain four matching entries in 2021–2024 and ten in 2025–2026. These are not ten simultaneously active codes. The [USITC July 1, 2025 change record](https://www.usitc.gov/tariff_affairs/documents/484%28f%29_committee_changes_for_july_1_2025-final.pdf), pages 1–3, discontinues `0901210035` in favor of `0901210015`, `0901210025`, `0901210040`, and `0901210045` in favor of `0901210020`, `0901210029`, `0901210049`, effective 2025-07-01. Every transfer remains inside 090121. This statistical detail split does not require a six-digit break. We use Census's six-digit aggregate rather than summing retained concordance entries.

The reviewed [January/February 2026](https://www.usitc.gov/tariff_affairs/documents/list_of_committee_changes_for_january-1-2026-and-february-1-2026.pdf) and [July 2026](https://www.usitc.gov/tariff_affairs/documents/list_of_committee_changes_for_july_1_2026-final.pdf) change records contain no 0901 match. We have not exhaustively inspected every historical intrayear HTS revision; annual concordances are reference snapshots, not validity calendars. This review establishes classification comparability within the stated range, not reporting continuity, revision stability, or completeness of country observations.

The manual workflow passes [the reviewed interval file](../../data/classification/090121.json) through `--classification`. The CLI clips evidence to an earlier reporting month and never extends it beyond 2026-08. Adjacent incompatible intervals remain separate. Months outside reviewed evidence are labeled unverified, and comparisons crossing them remain unavailable.

A successful mocked refresh verifies recovery and revision detection. It does not replace verification across two successive official monthly releases.
