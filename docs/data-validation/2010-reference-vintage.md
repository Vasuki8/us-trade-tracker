The [Mexico goods table](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/balance/c2010.html) reports nominal, unadjusted USD millions to one decimal. Its headings say Exports/Imports; the FAS total-export and general-import/customs linkage comes from official Census-basis definitions in the FT900 explanatory notes. Every dated comparison uses Supplement Exhibit 4's monthly FAS-export and customs-import columns, excluding adjacent YTD/annual and CIF columns.

Comparisons use exact integer dollars and a $100,000 display quantum. Signed unrounded differences are retained. An exact half-quantum tie would remain unavailable without an established publisher tie rule; none occurs here.

Seven national PDFs were authenticated and reused, and 17 missing issues plus two current snapshots were captured. A second reviewer independently authenticated all 26 originals, read all 24 table layouts and reproduced every control, source/page binding and outcome.

**Vintage and cause remain unestablished.** Dated URLs, titles and retrieval headers do not prove unchanged original-publication bytes. Current-table agreement does not establish a uniform archive revision vintage, and a difference is not automatically attributed to a revision.

The [evidence record](2010-reference-vintage-evidence.json) contains all 48 controls, differences, source hashes and physical/printed locators. The [classification findings](2010-classification-review.md) and [remaining acceptance gates](2010-acceptance-readiness.md) are separate.
