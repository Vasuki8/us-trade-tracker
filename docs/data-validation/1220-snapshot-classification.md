# Canada snapshot classification evidence

The separate [classification ledger](1220-snapshot-classification-evidence.json) covers the fixed 17 ordinary direction/HS6 candidates for September 2024–August 2026. All remain **unverified**. This records the available evidence and the exact missing proof; it does not claim that their definitions changed. The three known statistical categories remain separately excluded from ordinary comparisons: imports `980100`, exports `988000` and exports `880000`.

The root inspected unchanged Census historical-code workbooks, the previously retained annual concordance screens, and newly retained 2024, 2025 and 2026 Schedule B chapters 27, 71, 84, 87 and 88. Section XVI and XVII notes and the three cumulative add/delete lists were also retained and inspected. The chapter subheadings identify the selected families; the ledger names the exact PDF pages and original bytes/hash for each source. Each capture has separate request metadata in ignored `data/backups/canada-country-audit/classification-references-2026-10-11/`. Six initial abbreviated section URLs returned 404; official index links supplied the successful `section16.pdf` and `section17.pdf` captures. These are classification references, not trade dataset requests.

The [2024](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2024/index.html) and [2025](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2025/index.html) browsing indexes identify July editions. An annual URL, unchanged parent description, detailed-code validity dates or a cumulative change list cannot alone prove each intermediate month's effective semantic scope. Imports additionally need their direction-specific HTS definitions and notes, rather than treating export Schedule B as equivalent. [USITC's edition archive](https://www.usitc.gov/harmonized_tariff_information/hts/archive/list) is the primary route for that missing review.

The preserved historical screens identify in-window boundaries for import `271012` and `870829`, and export `271012` and `271019`. Their obsolete/replacement detailed codes share HS6 prefixes, but no predecessor allocation or unchanged-parent-scope conclusion is inferred from that fact. The ledger retains literal `Current` and `Prior to 2007` meanings in its source references; neither becomes an invented exact date. Other families also remain unverified until effective definitions, relevant notes and transfer history are sufficiently established. Statistical capture flags and accepted coffee classification evidence are unchanged.

Read-only `pypdf` extraction locates parent text and dated changes; original PDFs are unchanged. Historical XLSX inspection uses the original worksheet/shared-string XML, preserving malformed core-property metadata. No new parser dependency is used by the assessment command. An independent whole-branch reviewer must inspect these conclusions and source identities before integration. A compatible ledger entry requires scope evidence and dated-change evidence; ordinary month/rolling comparisons stay null under the current unverified ledger, while retained dollar observations remain inspectable. Public availability remains false regardless of classification status.
