## Review Focus

1. `snapshot` reaches a reader that accepts only `fixture`/`selected`, or a private flag is empty: map explicitly to `selected`, reject crossed configuration before reads and refuse crossed reader results. Task 1 owns the regressions.
2. Extending an accepted/pilot branch changes old context bytes or grants a statistical comparison: authenticate saved old renderings/CSVs, compare every new original column independently and retain unavailable blanks. Tasks 2 and 4 own the checks.
3. A ZIP contains a wrapper directory, extra/duplicate/traversal/symlink entry, stale bytes, or an oversized/control file: reject it before a success receipt or upload; count controls and authenticate readback contents. Task 4 owns negative probes.
4. Localhost hides Pages-specific behavior: absence of a top-level 404 yields an unwanted 200 homepage, or `_headers` is ignored. Tasks 3/4 check the package; Task 6 must verify real status, body and headers at the live origins.
5. An uncertain upload or failed live check is labeled published, or a canonical replacement is called complete withdrawal while old URLs remain: inspect the existing provider operation, preserve partial outcomes, and distinguish all URL states and authorized responses. Tasks 5 and 6 own evidence/operational checks.
