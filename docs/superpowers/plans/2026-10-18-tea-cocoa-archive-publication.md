## Intent, recommendation and alternatives

The requested result is two more trustworthy product profiles with their reported partner-country data, using the existing imports/exports, country changes, estimated duties, briefings and downloads. It is not another product intake or a Mexico country profile. Success means exact accepted source/archive/package bindings, useful concise pages, unchanged coffee/Canada values, verified public behavior and actual cleanup.

**Recommended:** preserve and accept the already verified candidates. Existing `restore`/`stage`/`verify`/`promote` commands support this without re-fetching Census. Add only artifact transport/identity gates and a two-phase workflow; keep the archive protocol unchanged.

Alternatives considered: the existing initialization import would obtain different captures, spend another source allowance and require fresh independent arithmetic; it is unnecessary here. Publishing local candidates without verified durable acceptance would bypass the backup/recovery gate. Neither alternative is authorized by this plan.
