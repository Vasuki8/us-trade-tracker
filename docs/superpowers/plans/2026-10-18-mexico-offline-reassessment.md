## Recommendation and alternatives

The intended user outcome is useful country-wide research backed by complete, qualified evidence. Mexico is already reported as a partner in the four published product profiles; a future country-wide profile would answer a different question about all U.S. goods trade with Mexico.

Recommend closing Mexico's existing feasibility gap before proposing another product cohort or speculative interface changes. Tea and cocoa have already broadened the product pilot. No new user-session or demand evidence identifies a higher-value cohort or interface feature. Owner-run pilot validation remains useful in parallel, but recruitment, interviews and participant data are outside this batch.

The three approaches considered are: (1) complete Mexico's retained-source feasibility, recommended because it addresses a concrete unfinished gate with no new data request; (2) add another named product cohort, deferred until there is a clearer user need; (3) change the interface, deferred unless a reproduced defect or observed task difficulty identifies the change. Completing Mexico arithmetic still does not establish classification continuity, current-reference agreement or commercial demand.
