## How to interpret and reproduce the example

Duty share is `100 × CAL_DUT_MO / CON_VAL_MO`. A rolling share uses the **ratio of sums** over the same complete period, never an average of monthly percentages. The RP70/79 percentage has a different denominator: affected dutiable value divided by matching `DUT_VAL_MO`. Every positive affected value in the tables qualifies its period; the roasted August 2026 affected value is a reported zero, not an unavailable value. The monthly roasted comparison still includes a qualified prior period.

RP70/79 can report dutiable value without calculating duty. Reported zero duties do not prove duty-free treatment. Calculated duties can differ from amounts actually paid, exclude antidumping and countervailing duties, and do not establish final shipment liability, later refunds or the cause of a tariff change. Composition and reporting coverage can affect changes. Country share uses matching merchandise world totals; the duty denominator is consumption value. They are different measures.

These examples have complete compatible periods. Missing/suppressed observations are not imputed as zero; incomplete or classification-incompatible comparisons remain unavailable. Zero or missing consumption value has no duty share, and a zero or missing prior amount has no growth percentage. Current retained validation and historical extra28 evidence support the existing acceptance; the extra28 producer binding remains indirect. This preparation is not a fresh audit of the official source or a claim that every country is comparable.
