## Meaning and limits

Recorded keys compare an observation with the previous retained release within overlapping history. They can represent additions, removals and amount or status changes. They do not provide old values, dates, causes or cumulative revision history. No mark does not mean never revised or final; an initial snapshot may lack a baseline. A removed monthly observation can remain unavailable and marked when its series remains selectable. Entirely removed countries are not reconstructed.

The two accepted coffee releases currently have no merchandise or duty revision keys. Retained acceptance therefore verifies no invented badges or open revised points. Positive marks use explicitly synthetic fixtures only. Estimated-duty per-measure attribution, new source retrieval, accepted-data replacement, further intake and R2 operations are excluded. Pilot-demand evidence and successive-official-release acceptance remain open.
